About
AKNACK Tool Cheongdam · Hair & Makeup Salon
K-entertainment field expertise
for complete hair & makeup
Combining Cheongdam-dong’s luxurious atmosphere with AKNACK’s hip sensibility, we complete everything from hair to makeup in one visit.

WHY CHEONGDAM
Why choose AKNACK Tool Cheongdam
01
Your own style completed by entertainment and advertising field designers
AKNACK Cheongdam is a total beauty salon staffed by multi-talented designers who also work on entertainment and advertising schedules. With an eye proven on stages and photoshoot sets, they complete a style just for you.
02
Hair & makeup finished in one visit
You can receive both hair and makeup services in one place. No need to book separately or move around—complete your entire styling in a single visit.
03
Cheongdam luxury + AKNACK’s unique sensibility
This space adds AKNACK’s hip sensibility to Cheongdam-dong’s luxurious atmosphere. It is a great fit for those who want both elegance and individuality.
04
A salon known by word of mouth for its haircut skills
AKNACK is known as a salon that excels at haircuts. Rather than flashy techniques seen on social media, it values the fundamentals of haircutting most. Texture, silhouette, and movement are considered together to create a style that suits your face shape and head shape. Dedicated men’s designers are also on site, so male customers can receive professional care.
05
Head spa care that reaches the scalp
A scalp head spa using Jennymori equipment is also available. With deep scalp cleansing and massage before and after treatments, you can care for both your hair and scalp at the same time.
✈️ TRAVELER SPECIAL PROMOTION
Enjoy a K-Beauty package at an even better value during your trip to Korea!
Specially designed for travelers, this package lets you enjoy a hair service and care treatment in one visit.
Enjoy a better value than booking each service separately, plus special benefits exclusively for travelers.
SERVICE ENVIRONMENT
Comfortable service environment
To help international customers use the service comfortably, all menus are also written in English, and basic English conversation guides are prepared inside the shop for smoother communication.
In addition to various treatments such as perms, coloring, and care, you can enjoy a comfortable experience with systematic service from consultation through the treatment process.
