About

🌿 The Brand Philosophy of 'Sanho Mansion'

'Sanho Mansion' is an emotive photo studio where Korea's analog sensibility and contemporary aesthetics harmonize. This place is not just somewhere to take pictures; it offers a moment to record the truest version of "who I am now."

In particular, 'ID photo' sessions go beyond mere document photos &mdash; they are about finding the most authentic moments amid the ever-changing expressions and emotions of everyday life. A single shot captured under soft, warm lighting becomes a memory that lingers in the heart long after time passes.

The 'Random Gift' session is a special shoot without preset concepts or poses, completed by the natural blend of that day's light, air, and emotions. With fresh flowers personally selected by Sanho Mansion and an added analog sensibility, the day's atmosphere is recorded like a work of art.

Ultimately, 'Sanho Mansion' strives not for the "perfect photo" but for beautifully preserving the moments that feel most like you. Here, you will rediscover "the you within Korea."

An Authentic Local Experience

Located near Jongno 3-ga Station, where Korean tradition and modernity coexist, after your shoot you can also enjoy nearby attractions like Ikseon-dong, Insadong, and Changdeokgung.
