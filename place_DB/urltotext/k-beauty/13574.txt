About

The Hair & Makeup Salon Trusted by BLACKPINK, NCT, IVE, and More

Trusted by actors Yoo Tae-oh, Heo Sung-tae, and many more, Soonsoo offers natural, sophisticated, camera-ready K-Beauty hair and makeup.

💄 Premium K-Beauty in Cheongdam

Professional artists enhance each client's natural charm with camera-ready styling.
