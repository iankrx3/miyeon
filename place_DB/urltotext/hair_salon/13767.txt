About
🧬 Personalized Scalp Care
Moclock knows that early action is everything when it comes to hair loss. On your first visit, a scalp magnified photo and condition analysis are performed, and the system recommends a program that perfectly matches the customer's scalp type and concerns.
Treatments like Hair Loss Care and Power Scalp Scaling nourish the scalp and boost elasticity, making them especially effective for hair loss, dandruff, itchiness, oiliness, and seborrheic dermatitis.
The full-course experience covers everything from a nape and trapezius massage to collagen packs, carbonated head spa, galvanic massage, and LED therapy.
🌏
Designed to Be Easy for Foreigners
All treatments take place in a private room, and everything from consultation to shampooing is handled in one space, so there's no need to worry about language barriers or other guests.
Scalp care and a shoulder-and-neck massage in one session means you can recover from travel fatigue and take care of your hair health at the same time.
It's a 1–2 minute walk from Nonhyeon Station Exit 3, so it fits naturally into a day of shopping or sightseeing in Gangnam.
K-pop idols and Korean influencers are known to visit regularly, and every guest receives the same level of care.
✨ Creatrip Booking Benefits
Book through Creatrip and get a professional scalp diagnosis and consultation worth ₩30,000 included for free, with no extra back-and-forth needed to complete your reservation.
Moclock Gangnam operates on a 100% appointment basis, so every guest gets a proper one-on-one consultation and a personalized treatment plan without feeling rushed.
