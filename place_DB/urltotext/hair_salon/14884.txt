About
Why We Recommend It
💇🏻‍♀️ Customized Makeover system to enhance procedure results
This isn't just a hair salon. It's a full image studio where everything is designed around your total look. You can get personal color analysis, hair consultation, makeup, perm, and color all in one place, all in one visit.
No need to book hair and makeup separately. You can get a complete makeover in one appointment, which is perfect if you have a special event, photoshoot, or just want to look your best while traveling.
They also offer customized scalp care programs designed to address hair concerns at the root. It's not just about how you look today, but about improving your hair health long-term.
English consultations are available throughout the salon, so international guests can communicate comfortably and get the same level of professional service.
🌿 Convenient location and accessibility in central Seoul
Located in the heart of Seoul, you're within easy reach of Gyeongbokgung Palace, Gwanghwamun, Cheongwadae, and the charming Seochon neighborhood.
It's a great spot to get your hair and makeup done between sightseeing, or before an important meeting. And since the area blends tradition with modern Seoul vibes, it's especially convenient if you're planning a hanbok experience or a photo session nearby.
