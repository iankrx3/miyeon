About
Why We Recommend It
💇🏼‍♀️ Seoul’s go-to spot for K-Beauty hair trends
Soonsiki is Creatrip's most-booked hair salon, with thousands of international guests every month.
Korean models and celebrities come here for the latest trends executed with precision.
Expect trendsetting K-Beauty cuts, color, and styling at the highest level.
Transparent pricing means you'll know the exact cost before your appointment—no surprise fees.
🌏
Foreigner-friendly location in trendy Seongsu
SOONSIKI Seongsu is staffed by designers with extensive experience serving international clients. The brand's years of working with global guests means you can expect reliable, professional service.
The salon sits in Seongsu-dong, Seoul's trendiest neighborhood packed with fashion boutiques, pop-up stores, and Instagram-worthy cafes. Perfect for combining your hair appointment with shopping or exploring the area.
Beyond cuts and color, the salon offers head spa treatments, scalp care, and professional personal color analysis to match your hairstyle with your natural palette.
✨ Creatrip exclusive reservation benefits
Book through Creatrip and leave a review to save up to 38% (haircuts excluded).
With hundreds of verified reviews, you can trust the quality—and the savings.
Head Spa Types
1. 8-Step Head Spa (Half Course)
A quick scalp detox treatment including scaling, seated shampoo, and tonic application to cleanse the scalp and remove toxins.
Process:
Scalp analysis → Scaling application → Scalp detox → Seated shampoo → Galvanic toxin removal → Rinse → Tonic application → Galvanic massage → Cool air drying → Final styling → Before & after scalp check
2. 16-Step Head Spa (Full Course)
A deep scalp care treatment combining scaling, aromatherapy, detox, heated lymph massage, and galvanic massage for a thorough cleansing and relaxation experience.
Process:
Scalp analysis → Scalp aromatherapy → Scalp detox → Scaling application → Massage oil therapy → Heated trapezius massage → Lymph massage → Seated shampoo → Galvanic toxin removal → Rinse (Dohi Spa) → Tonic application → Galvanic massage → Cool air drying → Final styling → Before & after scalp check
Kérastase Treatment Options
1. Acuemdu Filler Care
A special treatment by Acuemdu with a 3-steps formula designed for damaged hair.
Keratin – Strengthens hair by replenishing proteins and forming a protective layer.
Micron Collagen & Natural Oils – A blend of argan and jojoba oils for deep nourishment.
Cream Balm Treatment – Provides protection from the cuticle to the core using natural ingredients.
2. Kérastase Basic Care
A 2-step care system with Kérastase shampoo and treatment, customized to your hair condition.
Quick and effective, ideal for dry, severely damaged, or frizzy hair.
3. Kérastase Full Care
A 4-step intensive treatment including Kérastase shampoo, ampoule, booster, and treatment.
Helps protect and restore hair before and after coloring or perm treatments.
4. Kérastase Full + Scalp Cleansing
A complete care program combining deep scalp cleansing, Kérastase shampoo, ampoule, booster, and scalp & hair treatment.
Cleanses the scalp, adds hydration, and promotes healthier hair growth.
Provides deep moisture and nutrients for shiny, strong, and long-lasting healthy hair.
