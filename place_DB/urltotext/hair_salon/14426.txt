About
💇🏼‍♀️ Trendy Color and Bleaching Treatment Salon
We specialize in trendy bleaching and color treatments, popular among Seoul's younger crowd, with a full range of bold and vibrant color options. Whether you're looking for the latest Korean trends or bold, unique colors, we've got you covered.
We offer personalized consultations to find the perfect style for your face shape and aesthetic, bringing your vision to life.
Using premium brands like L'Oréal, Wella, and Milbon, we minimize damage while achieving vivid, beautiful color. Personalized hair treatments keep your hair healthy between visits.
🌏
Designed for International Guests
Our English-speaking stylists and English price list ensure smooth, comfortable service with no language barriers.
Just a short walk from Hongik Univ. Station, making it easy to stop by before or after exploring Hongdae's trendy cafes and shopping.
