Proven Expertise and Extensive Experience

Abijou Clinic has been at the forefront of Korean aesthetic medicine since 2006. Our team of doctors, each with 20+ years of hands-on experience, delivers everything from laser treatments and skin lifting to Botox, minor procedures, and body contouring, all under one roof.

With over 500 Botox sessions and 300 laser treatments performed every single day, we're one of the busiest and most trusted dermatology clinics in Korea. That kind of volume means you're in experienced hands.

Built for International Visitors

Visiting a clinic in a foreign country can be stressful &mdash; Abijou has made it easy.

60 in-house interpreters covering 9 languages : English, Japanese, Chinese, Thai, Vietnamese, Mongolian, French, Portuguese & Spanish &mdash; so you'll never feel lost

Luggage storage available on-site: Drop your bags and head straight to your appointment

2 minutes from Euljiro 1-ga Station (Exit 6) : right in the heart of Seoul

Foreigners pay the same price as locals. No hidden fees, no international pricing. Abijou has maintained this policy for 20 years along with a strict commitment to using only verified products in the correct dosages, every single time.
