TRUST POINT

What Makes Arumdaun Nara Dermatology Stand Out

25 years at Gangnam Station This is a dermatology specialty clinic that has been operating at Gangnam Station for 25 years.

Backed by early adoption in Korea of Thermage and Ulthera and long-standing Lifting expertise, we provide personalized care based on your individual skin condition.

All medical staff are board-certified dermatologists All physicians are board-certified dermatologists, representing about 2% of doctors in Korea, and they provide direct care from consultation through treatment.

Research-based care Based on 50+ SCI-level papers and journal publications related to skin procedures, including Thermage FLX, Ulthera, Acne and Pores, as well as six Minister’s Awards, the clinic provides evidence-based care.

On-site interpretation staff Japanese and Chinese interpreters are on-site, and there are four Japanese coordinators on staff who support you in your language, from consultation to post-visit precautions before you head home.

Safe medical care Native-language interpretation helps ensure smooth communication, and the clinic is enrolled in overseas patient medical malpractice liability insurance, so international visitors can receive treatment with peace of mind.

Direct care from consultation to aftercare The director personally checks everything from consultation to treatment and post-treatment follow-up, so you feel truly cared for even after just one visit.

Convenience to match your travel schedule Located right in front of Exit 1 of Gangnam Station, the clinic is open on Sundays and public holidays, making it easy to fit into your itinerary. Water, drinks, and light snacks are available on-site.

TREATMENT INFORMATION

Detailed Information by Procedure

Thermage FLX 600 shots + Rejuran HB 2cc

Details Thermage FLX uses monopolar radiofrequency to deliver heat into the dermal layer, promoting Collagen production and helping improve skin firmness, Pores, and skin texture. Rejuran HB is a procedure that helps with skin regeneration and hydration, fine lines, and improving the skin barrier.

Duration

120 minutes

Effects

Thermage: 6 months to 1 year / Rejuran HB: 3 months

Recommended For

Those who want to improve nasolabial folds, a double chin, and overall skin elasticity

Precautions You can wash your face and apply makeup starting the next day.

Avoid alcohol and smoking for about 5 to 7 days.

Redness, swelling, and mild pain may last for a few days after the procedure. If symptoms are severe, please contact the clinic.

Bruising, redness, and small bumps (embossing) may appear at the Rejuran injection sites and can last about 5 to 7 days.

XERF 600 shots

Details XERF is a monopolar radiofrequency device that uses two frequencies (6.78MHz and 2MHz). It is a non-invasive RF energy treatment that stably delivers RF heat energy deep into the skin to help refine overall skin condition.

Duration

90 minutes

Effects

6 months to 1 year

Recommended For

Those who need Collagen regeneration throughout all skin layers, those looking for a treatment with less discomfort, and those who want to improve fine lines on the neck

Precautions You can wash your face and apply makeup starting the day of the procedure.

Avoid alcohol and smoking for about 5 to 7 days.

Redness and swelling may last for a few days after the procedure. If symptoms are severe, please contact the clinic.

Skin Botox + Modeling Pack

Details This procedure involves injecting Botox very superficially into the skin to help refine Pores and improve skin texture.

Duration

90 minutes

Effects

3 months to 6 months

Recommended For

Those who want to improve Pores and skin texture

Precautions You can wash your face and apply makeup starting the next day.

Avoid alcohol and smoking for about 5 to 7 days.

Bruising, redness, and small bumps (embossing) may appear at the injection sites, bruising can last about 5 to 7 days.

Sofwave 100 shots + Rejuran HB 2cc

Details Sofwave is an ultrasound-based procedure that uses SUPERB technology to deliver ultrasound energy in seven parallel thermal columns at the same time, distributing heat broadly and evenly across the dermal layer. It helps with skin tightening and improving fine lines. Rejuran HB helps activate skin regeneration and supports hydration, fine lines, and improving the skin barrier.

Duration

120 minutes

Effects

Sofwave: 6 months to 1 year / Rejuran HB: 3 months

Recommended For

Those concerned about sunken cheeks, those who want to improve skin texture as well, and those worried about sagging after contouring surgery or Liposuction

Precautions You can wash your face and apply makeup starting the next day.

Avoid alcohol and smoking for about 5 to 7 days.

Redness, swelling, and mild pain may last for a few days after the procedure. If symptoms are severe, please contact the clinic.

In some cases, scratch marks, blisters, or severe swelling may occur, if so, please contact the clinic.

Bruising, redness, and small bumps (embossing) may appear at the Rejuran injection sites and can last about 5 to 7 days.
