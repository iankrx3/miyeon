About




Creatrip Exclusive Benefits

Book a Creatrip-exclusive package and access treatments at a better price, matched to your skin concerns.
Not sure which procedure to get? You can book a consultation first and decide on-site, with zero pressure.




A treatment plan tailored to your skin condition, with expert care

Based on each customer’s skin condition and concerns, the medical team suggests a customized treatment plan and provides careful, detailed care.
In addition to single packages, the clinic offers a variety of treatment lineups tailored to each patient’s condition, allowing customers to receive suitable treatments after a precise consultation.
For accurate skin analysis, customers are guided on how to cleanse their face before Mark-Vu imaging, and after the procedure, professional staff provide guidance on soothing and regeneration care.
For customers who are sensitive to pain, sleep anesthesia is available if needed, allowing them to receive treatments more comfortably.
The clinic is trusted by patients from various nationalities, and Korean patients in particular show very high satisfaction, leading to a high revisit rate.




Seamless Support for International Visitors, From Consultation to Aftercare

On-site staff speak Japanese, English, Mandarin, Cantonese, and Thai — covering consultations, payment, procedure details, and aftercare.
After your visit, you'll receive printed aftercare instructions in your language to take home.




Steps from Seomyeon Station — Easy to Reach, Even Mid-Trip

Vands Clinic Busan Seomyeon Branch is right in front of Seomyeon Station Exit 7 — straightforward to find, even on your first trip to Korea.
Luggage storage is available on-site, so you can stop by straight from the airport or before heading out.
Water, drinks, and light snacks are provided while you wait.

Reservation Info

Information

Same-day booking is available.
On-site support is available in English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese, Vietnamese, and Thai.
Your visit follows this flow: Consultation → Payment → Procedure → Aftercare guidance.
Post-treatment care guides are available in English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Thai, and Vietnamese.




How to Reserve

Fill in all required fields when booking.
Complete your free reservation.
Your booking will be confirmed within 1 business day of payment. If the date needs to change, you'll be notified via the email or contact information you provided.
At your visit, show your reservation details to a staff member at the front desk.




During Your Visit

Head to the 2nd floor and show your Creatrip reservation confirmation and passport at the front desk.
Take a seat in the lobby and wait to be called.
When your name is called, you'll be taken to a consultation room where staff will walk you through your procedure, including the steps involved and expected duration.
In the treatment room, you'll have a one-on-one consultation with the doctor before your procedure begins.
Your procedure will then be carried out.
Move to the aftercare room for post-treatment soothing and recovery care.
At the counter, you'll receive aftercare instructions and precaution details.
If a prescription is needed, a pharmacy is available nearby. Follow-up appointments can also be arranged.
Cancellation & Refund Policy
Free reservations can be canceled at no charge any time before your appointment date.
No-shows, same-day cancellations, and repeated cancellations may affect your ability to make future bookings.
If you can't make it, please cancel in advance.
Late arrivals and no-shows create difficulties for the clinic and other patients.
Things To Keep In Mind

Information to Note Before Your Visit

Please bring your passport for identity verification when visiting the clinic.
Tax refunds will end starting January 1, 2026. Please keep this in mind when making a reservation.
Depending on the clinic’s on-site situation, you may have to wait.
The procedure takes 1 to 2 hours.
For patients who are highly sensitive to pain, treatment can be done comfortably under safe sedation anesthesia if needed. (If you would like sedation anesthesia, please book a consultation first and arrange an additional schedule with the clinic.)
For Hair Removal customers, please shave the area the day before your visit, then come in.
If you will be receiving sedation anesthesia, please fast for at least 6 hours and come in with nail polish or gel nails removed from your fingernails. Please also stop taking blood pressure medication from 7 days before your visit.




Post-Procedure Precautions

Post-procedure precautions will be provided individually during your visit, depending on the procedure you selected. 
If you have any questions about your selected procedure, feel free to contact us at help@creatrip.com.
For inquiries, please contact help@creatrip.com.
All times are shown in Korea Standard Time (KST).
