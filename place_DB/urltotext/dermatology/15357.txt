Shurink Universe Ultra 300 shots

Duration

1 hour

Recommended For

Those who want to improve overall skin elasticity

Those who want to smooth uneven facial contours

Those looking for a lifting treatment that has minimal impact on daily life

Precautions by Procedure

You may wash your face and apply makeup on the day of the procedure. However, if your skin is sensitive or still red, we recommend applying makeup starting the next day.

For one week, avoid activities that generate heat, such as saunas, jjimjilbangs, and intense exercise.

For one week, avoid alcohol and smoking to support proper skin regeneration after the procedure.

You may experience temporary soreness, tightness, mild swelling, bruising, or redness after the procedure, which usually subsides naturally within a few days.

You may temporarily feel numbness, muscle aches, or pain after the procedure, which normally eases within a few days.

We recommend using sunscreen to protect skin that may be more sensitive to UV after the procedure.

Potenza

Duration

1 hour

Recommended For

Anyone who wants comprehensive care for complex skin concerns

Those worried about acne, scars, or enlarged pores

People looking to improve overall skin elasticity

Anyone who wants smooth, clear skin

Precautions

For the first 12&ndash;24 hours, cleanse gently with water or pat with a toner, avoid rubbing or exfoliation. We recommend applying makeup starting the next day.

Minimize touching the treated area and applying cosmetics while tiny micro-cracks are present.

Avoid skincare products containing retinol or vitamin C for one week.

If flaking occurs, do not pick at it, let it fall off naturally.

Depending on individual skin, you may notice temporary tip marks or redness at the treatment site. This is a normal process and will fade over time, so there is no need to worry.

PicoSure Toning

Duration

1 hour

Recommended For

Those concerned about skin pigmentation (melasma, freckles, blemishes, etc.)

Those who want to improve pores and acne scars at the same time

Those who want a quick procedure with minimal side effects

Precautions by Procedure

You may wash your face and apply makeup on the day of the procedure. However, if your skin is sensitive or still has redness, we recommend applying makeup starting the next day.

For one week, avoid activities that generate heat, such as saunas, jjimjilbangs, and intense exercise.

You may experience temporary slight redness or a warm, prickling sensation after the procedure, but these usually subside naturally within a few days.

Scabbing may occasionally occur after the procedure, but they will fall off naturally, so avoid picking them off with your hands.

Your skin may feel dry after the procedure, so use moisturizer frequently, and be sure to apply sunscreen when going outside.

Because your skin will be sensitive after the procedure, avoid using functional cosmetics that contain retinol or vitamin C.

Aqua Peeling

Duration

1 hour

Recommended For

Those suffering from frequent breakouts due to excessive sebum production

Those with blackheads

Those who want a gentle peel

Those whose makeup does not apply well or looks cakey

Precautions

When dead skin peels, avoid forcefully picking at it and let it shed naturally.

Sun exposure after peeling can cause melasma and dark spots, so we recommend using sunscreen.

Because the skin barrier is temporarily weakened after peeling, we recommend using moisturizing and regenerating creams.

When peeling and flakes occur, avoid forcibly removing them and let them shed naturally.

Since the skin barrier is temporarily weakened after peeling, use moisturizing and regenerative cream.
