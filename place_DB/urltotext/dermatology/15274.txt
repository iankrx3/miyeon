Procedures

⭐ TOP 5 POPULAR PACKAGES FOR INTERNATIONAL CUSTOMERS ⭐

1. ULTHERA (ULTHERAPY) + THERMAGE

Ulthera (Ultherapy) is an ultrasound lifting laser that tightens the deeper SMAS layer.

Thermage is a radiofrequency treatment that firms collagen and softens fine lines.

* Recommended for sagging jawline, cheeks, and overall decreased skin firmness.

2. COLLAGEN BOOSTER (Sculptra, Re2O)

Sculptra and Re2O are collagen‑stimulating injectable treatments that gradually restore volume and improve skin elasticity over time.

* Recommended for hollow or sunken areas and early signs of sagging where you want a natural, gradual lift.

3. SKIN BOOSTER (REJURAN, JUVELOOK) + POTENZA / DERMASHINE

Rejuran and Juvelook are regenerative and collagen‑stimulating skin booster injections, combined with Potenza microneedling RF or Dermashine injector for better delivery and collagen remodeling.

* Recommended for rough texture, enlarged pores, fine lines, and overall dull, tired skin.

4. ULTHERA (ULTHERAPY) + ONDA

Ulthera (Ultherapy) is a focused ultrasound lifting treatment that tightens deeper layers.

ONDA is a microwave‑based contouring device that helps reduce fat and refine the jawline.

* Recommended for double chin or lower face fullness with sagging jawline.

5. BOTOX

Botox is a neuromodulator injection that relaxes overactive muscles to soften expression lines such as frown lines, forehead wrinkles, and crow&rsquo;s feet.

* Recommended if you want smoother, less &lsquo;tired&rsquo; or &lsquo;angry&rsquo;‑looking lines with a quick, no‑downtime treatment.
