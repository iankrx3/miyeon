Reservation Info

Information

Please make your reservation at least 1 days in advance.
Language: English, Chinese 

How to Reserve

Please fill out all required information accurately on the reservation page.
Proceed with the free reservation. 
After payment, your reservation will be confirmed within 1–2 business days.
We’ll contact you via email or messenger if any changes to your reservation are necessary.
Show your reservation details to the staff when you arrive.

During Your Visit 

Proceed to the 8th floor and present your Creatrip reservation confirmation and passport at the front desk.
Please wait briefly in the designated waiting area.
When your name is called, you will be guided to the consultation room for a detailed explanation of the procedure.
Move to the treatment room for a personalized consultation with the doctor.
Receive your treatment as instructed by the medical staff.
Visit the aftercare room for detailed post-treatment guidance.
Return to the front desk to receive instructions on aftercare precautions.
If necessary, you may receive a prescription for medication or schedule a follow-up visit.

How to Get There

Cancellation & Refund Policy
Since it is a free reservation, you can cancel for free until the reservation date.
If you are unable to visit, please make sure to cancel your reservation before the appointment time.
For refunds on amounts paid at the hospital, please contact the hospital directly.
Things To Keep In Mind

Visit

Please bring your passport for identification when visiting the clinic.
For reservation changes or inquiries, contact help@creatrip.com.
If you would like sleep anesthesia, please contact help@creatrip.com first. Please note that choosing sleep anesthesia may require waiting due to preparation time.

Procedures

Avoid drinking alcohol and smoking for one week after treatment to ensure smooth skin recovery.
Temporary skin issues such as irritation, swelling, bruising, or redness may occur after treatment but should subside quickly. If symptoms persist, please contact help@creatrip.com.
