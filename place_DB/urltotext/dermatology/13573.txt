VISIA SKIN ANALYSIS A More Accurate Way to Understand Your Skin See hard-to-detect skin factors as measurable data to guide your consultation and treatment selection. View VISIA Analysis Details ＋ Why Choose a VISIA Analysis? It photographs and analyzes pores, oil levels, wrinkles, pigmentation, moisture, elasticity, skin tone, skin temperature, and other 8 skin factors to provide a detailed picture of your current skin condition.

The measurements reveal details that are difficult to assess visually, helping you understand the consultation and choose a treatment suited to your concerns.

STEP 1 Skin Imaging A high-resolution camera and multiple sensors capture your skin condition.

STEP 2 Analysis of 8 Skin Factors Pores, oil levels, wrinkles, pigmentation, moisture, elasticity, skin tone, and skin temperature are analyzed, with results available in about 10 minutes.

STEP 3 Personalized Treatment Recommendations Based on your results and individual skin concerns, the clinic recommends suitable medical aesthetic solutions.
