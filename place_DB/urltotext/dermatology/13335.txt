Why We Recommend It

A skin analysis is provided followed by a review of the results by an experienced consultant for a more personalized consultation.

The clinic specializes in lifting treatments such as thread lift and laser lift, which are especially popular among Koreans in their 20s up to 50s+ for its refined results and focus on safety.

International clients receive the same pricing as local clients, with occasional access to even better offers. All clients are treated equally, with no difference in service.

Located just a 3-minute walk from Gangnam Station Exits 9 and 10, the clinic is easy to access and convenient to visit during your trip.
