Procedure Details

Potenza (pumping tip) + Rejuran Healer 2cc + regenerative laser + cryo + modeling mask

Treatment Overview

Combines Potenza pumping tips, Rejuran Healer 2cc, regenerative laser, cryo therapy, and modeling mask to improve skin texture, pores, and elasticity

Duration

About 1 hour to 1 hour 30 minutes

Results Duration

1&ndash;3 months

Recommended For

People with enlarged pores or scars, rough skin, troubled skin, or dry skin

Post-Treatment Care

Avoid alcohol for one week

Apply sunscreen when outdoors

Wash your face with cool water.

Chin tip & jawline filler (Juvederm) 2cc + cobblestone chin Botox (Xeomin)

Treatment Overview

Tidy the chin point and jawline with Juvederm filler, and smooth a dimpled chin using Xeomin Botox to complete a V-line

Duration

Within 30 minutes

Results Duration

More than 1 year and 6 months

Recommended For

Those with a short chin, anyone who wants to create a V-line, and those who want to change their chin line with a simple procedure

Post-Treatment Care

Avoid alcohol for one week

Avoid putting pressure on the treated area for two weeks.

Lip Filler (Juvederm) 1cc + Corner-of-the-Mouth Botox (Xeomin)

Treatment Overview

Improve lip volume and shape with Juvederm filler, and lift drooping corners of the mouth with Xeomin Botox to create a natural mouth appearance

Duration

Under 30 minutes

Results Duration

Lasts more than 1 year

Recommended For

People with thin lips, those with asymmetrical lip shape, those with drooping mouth corners, anyone who wants to improve their smile with a simple procedure, and people who want to improve lip wrinkles and elasticity

Post-Treatment Care

Avoid alcohol for one week

Avoid using straws for two weeks, and try to avoid hot tea.

Ulthera Prime 300 shots + Densety Classic 300 shots

Treatment Overview

Combine Ulthera Prime and Densety Classic to lift from the deep layers of the skin and overall improve firmness, pores, and fine lines

Duration

60 minutes or more

Results Duration

From 6 months

Recommended For

People with skin sagging, concerns about pores or fine lines, or reduced skin elasticity

Precautions by Procedure

You may temporarily experience warmth, swelling, or skin tightness, but these will naturally fade over time.

Rejuran Healer 2cc

Treatment Overview

Inject Rejuran Healer into the skin to restore damaged skin and generally improve hydration, elasticity, and glow

Duration

Within 30 minutes

Results Duration

1&ndash;2 months

Recommended For

Those with dry, lackluster skin, thin or sensitive skin, enlarged pores, or uneven skin texture

Precautions

Embossing, redness, warmth, and slight swelling may occur, but these will naturally subside within 1&ndash;3 days.
