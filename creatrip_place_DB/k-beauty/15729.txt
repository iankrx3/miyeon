About
Myeongdong WELLNESS
In between your Myeongdong trip,
from foot baths to a K-beauty spa
A 3-minute walk from Myeongdong Station, enjoy a personalized experience in a private space, from foot baths and Korean-style body scrubs to scalp spa and facial skin care.
Highlights
A variety of wellness care in one building
It’s set up with a foot bath cafe on the 1st floor, and head spa, skin care, and body scrub spaces on the 2nd floor.
A convenient Myeongdong location for travelers
It’s about a 3-minute walk from Exit 4 of Myeongdong Station, and luggage storage is available.
Private care available for both men and women
You can receive treatments in a private room, and your companions can wait at the 1st-floor foot bath cafe.
Program Guide
Healing Combo: Foot Bath + Massage Chair + K-Dessert & Drink · 40 min
A relaxing course where you enjoy a warm foot bath and a full-body massage chair at the same time, then take a break with Korean-style dessert and a drink.
Steps
Foot bath and premium full-body massage chair simultaneously
Chewy Korean traditional rice cakes (tteok) provided
Choose 1 cafe drink
Enjoy your dessert and drink comfortably during the massage
Recommended for
Those with tired legs after shopping in Myeongdong
Those who want a private, cozy break during a busy trip
Those who want to try Korean traditional desserts along with cafe drinks
K-Ritual Body Scrub Spa (8-step course) · 60 min
An 8-step body care program that reinterprets Korea’s traditional body-scrub culture into modern spa therapy in a private space.
Steps
Undress & Water Shower
Warm Bath Soak
Korean Body Scrub
Body Wash & Rinse
Facial Cleansing & Mask Sheet Application
Hair Shampoo & Treatment
Mask Removal, Face & Hair Touch-Up
Body Lotion Application & Body Drying Finish
Recommended for
Those who want to experience Korea’s traditional body scrub in a modern spa style, in a private and cozy space rather than a public bathhouse
Important
Body scrub services are available to women only.

All-Tune’s premium anti-aging ritual combines Oxygen Therapy, HIFU, HiLDM water-drop ultrasound, RF, and Jade Therapy in one treatment.
This systematic 22-step care refines and cleanses the skin while the synergy of oxygen energy, HIFU, HiLDM, and RF helps improve firmness and facial contours from the deeper layers of the skin and supports collagen production.
It replenishes moisture and nutrients, helps soothe the skin for a clear and healthy-looking complexion, and extends care beyond the face to the scalp, neck, and shoulders. Refined technology and premium therapy come together in All-Tune’s signature anti-aging ritual for balanced, natural beauty.
