About

Why CCLIME Seongsu?

CCLIME is Korea's leading facial contouring spa, trusted by celebrities and beauty insiders for its expert hand techniques that sculpt and define your jawline, cheekbones, and facial balance—no machines, just skilled touch.

Each treatment includes a 15ml high-concentration lifting ampoule designed to firm, contour, and brighten your skin in a single session. You'll also receive skincare samples to take home so results last longer.

All therapists are certified in CCLIME's signature manual contouring method and trained in both technique and hospitality, ensuring every visit feels personalized and luxurious, whether you're at any other location.

Located in Seoul's trendy Seongsu district, known for its stylish cafes and boutique shops. Easy to pair your treatment with shopping or exploring the area.

Book through Creatrip and unlock exclusive perks: sample packs, V-Lift mask sheets—only available when you leave a review!
