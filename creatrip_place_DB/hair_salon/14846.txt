About
AKNACK Seongsu · Hair Salon
A detail-focused hair salon remembered not for Seongsu vibes,
but for its haircut skills
There is a reason Seongsu locals keep coming back. We start with a different approach to the cut, tailored to your face shape and head shape.
CREATRIP BENEFIT
✨Creatrip Exclusive Reservation Benefit
After paying through Creatrip, you can receive a discount when you write a photo review. (Discount benefits are provided only when a photo review is submitted.)
WHY SEONGSU
✂️ A haircut-focused salon loved by Seongsu hipsters
01
A salon serious about haircuts
AKNACK Seongsu is a salon known among Seongsu locals as a place that is great at haircuts. Rather than relying on SNS aesthetics, it stands out through the essentials of hair: excellent cutting skills.
02
Young, trendy Seongsu sensibility
With designers who understand Seongsu’s uniquely young and trendy vibe, the salon recommends styles that follow trends while still feeling like your own.
03
Specialized men’s hair care
A designer dedicated to men’s hair is on site. Men’s styles such as slick cuts and texture cuts can also be handled professionally.
04
Reliable designer expertise
The head director and deputy-director-level designers work together, so you can receive consistent service without large differences in skill level.
05
Jennymori scalp head spa
A scalp head spa using Jennymori equipment is also available. With deep scalp cleansing and massage before and after treatments, you can care for both your hair and scalp at the same time.
✈️ TRAVELER SPECIAL PROMOTION
Enjoy a K-Beauty package at an even better value during your trip to Korea!
Specially designed for travelers, this package lets you enjoy a hair service and care treatment in one visit.
Enjoy a better value than booking each service separately, plus special benefits exclusively for travelers.
SERVICE ENVIRONMENT
🌎 Comfortable service environment
To help international customers feel comfortable, all menus are also written in English, and basic English conversation guides are available inside the shop for smoother communication.
From perms, color, and care treatments to consultation and the treatment process, you can enjoy a comfortable visit with systematic service.

Scalp Scaling 6 Steps
View Details
[Step 1] Scalp Diagnosis
A detailed scalp analysis checks the condition of the pores and scalp, followed by a personalized 1:1 care plan for oily, dry, seborrheic, or sensitive scalp types.
[Step 2] Aroma Therapy
This step uses natural aroma oil to help remove toxins from the scalp and relieve stress.
[Step 3] Bionic LED Brush
Using microcurrent, this helps the previously applied aroma oil absorb better. Scalp gua sha relaxes the scalp, promotes smoother blood circulation, and helps release toxins.
[Step 4] Jennymori Scaling (Deep Pore Cleansing)
Made with natural mint extract and rosemary extract, this acidic peeling gel deep-cleanses pores without irritation while helping moisturize and soothe the scalp.
[Step 5] Mist
A low-temperature steam mist is applied for about 10 minutes to help soften sebum and dead skin cells together with the scaling solution, while supporting scalp hydration and circulation.
[Step 6] Jennymori Shampoo
A scalp-care shampoo containing functional ingredients such as biotin, mint, and panthenol, plus human stem cell culture medium.
3-Step Hair Care
View Details
[Step 0] AYUNCHE Shampoo
With pseudo-cuticle and amino-acid-based cleansing ingredients, this shampoo coats the surface of tangled, rough, and stiff hair, leaving it glossy and smooth to the touch.

An amino-acid blend helps repair the inside of the hair, creating healthy, even texture through to the ends.
[Step 2] NO.2 (Hydrate)
Coats the hair surface to leave hair feeling moisturized.
[Step 3] NO.3 (Top Coat)
Adds another protective coating to the hair surface for a smoother finish.
