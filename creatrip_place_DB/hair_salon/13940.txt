About
Why We Recommend It
✨Creatrip exclusive booking benefit
Pay through Creatrip and leave a review to receive an additional 10% off all services except haircuts!
💇🏼‍♀️ Personal consulting customized styling
ONYAD HAIR Seongsu specializes in trendy, hip styles with personalized consultations to find the perfect look for you.
We start by analyzing your face shape, proportions, features, and personal style to create a cohesive look through customized cuts and color.
We offer a full range of services including cuts, perms, color, and hair treatments, with meticulous attention to detail to match your personal image and style.
🌎 International Visitor-Friendly Services
English-speaking staff are available on-site to ensure smooth communication throughout your consultation and service.
Located just a 1-minute walk from Seoul Forest Station, the salon is easily accessible and perfect for exploring the trendy Seongsu neighborhood—enjoy local cafes, shops, and restaurants after your appointment.
