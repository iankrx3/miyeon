CANCELLATION POLICY
Policy
You can change or cancel your reservation up to 3 days before your visit (100% refund).
              Changes or cancellations are not allowed from 2 days before your visit (no refund).
              If you arrive more than 10–15 minutes late, your reservation will be automatically canceled and no refund will be provided.
info
PRECAUTIONS
Notes
Services are only available at the location you reserved. Please double-check the branch before visiting, as there may be multiple branches in the same area.

              Reservation changes and cancellations are only allowed up to 3 days before the appointment. Cancellations made after that are non-refundable.

              The listed prices are average prices for each service. Additional charges may apply on-site depending on hair length, damage level, or the assigned designer (paid on-site).

              Staff members are available who can provide basic support in English, Japanese, and Chinese. Translation tools may be used if needed to communicate specific requests.

              If you don’t have a specific style in mind, the designer can recommend a hairstyle based on your face shape and features.

              Please book the correct number of services according to the number of visitors. Booking two or more services per person is allowed.
