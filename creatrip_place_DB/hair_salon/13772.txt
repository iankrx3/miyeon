About
💇🏼‍♂️
Specializing in men's cuts and creative color
This salon focuses on men's cuts, perms, and color services tailored to your personal color palette.
Whether you want a clean everyday look or something bold and trendy, they've got you covered.
Not sure what works for you? The stylists will walk you through options based on your personal coloring, skin tone, and vibe during a consultation.
They use L'Oréal Professionnel and Milbon products for rich, natural-looking color. Plus, they'll treat your hair to keep it healthy and help the color last longer.
🌏 The Best Foreigner-Friendly Salon in Korea
The whole team speaks English, so you can easily explain what you're looking for without worrying about the language barrier.
The stylists have worked with international trends and diverse hair types, so they can suggest styles that actually fit your face shape and personality, not just what's popular in Korea right now.
They do balayage, highlights, ombre, and color correction. And they know how to work with all hair textures, whether yours is fine, thick, straight, or curly.
It's a 5-minute walk from Hongdae Station, so you can swing by before or after exploring the area.
