About
💇🏼‍♀️
A Salon Specializing in Trendy Color & Bleaching Services
We specialize in the bold, fashion-forward styles that younger clients love — from the latest K-beauty trends to one-of-a-kind colors and styles tailored just for you.
Every visit starts with a personalized hair consultation that takes your face shape and personal aesthetic into account, helping you achieve a look that feels naturally you.
Using premium products from L'Oréal, Wella, and Milbon, we minimize damage while delivering vivid, long-lasting color, along with customized treatment care to keep your hair healthy and strong.
🌏 A Salon Where Every Guest Feels Welcome
Our English-speaking stylists and English price list mean you can book, consult, and walk out looking great and completely barrier-free.
Conveniently located in Yeonnam-dong, just a short walk from Hongik Univ. Station, we're the perfect spot for anyone looking to express their individuality in a youthful, trend-forward space.
