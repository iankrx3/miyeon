About

Korea's Leading Zero Drug Loss Rejuran Clinic

Project U Clinic is an official Rejuran Advisory Clinic and Pharma Research KOL-designated practice, led by Dr. Lee Kyunghwan (Yonsei University College of Medicine, Severance Hospital).

What makes Project U's Rejuran different:

The clinic's proprietary Zero Drug Loss Rejuran technique addresses the four biggest problems with conventional Rejuran treatments &mdash; pain, drug loss, downtime, and inconsistent results.

1. Precision Delivery: All syringes use custom 2mm dermal-layer microneedles (35% thinner than standard), ensuring every drop reaches the dermis with zero waste.

2. 4D Pain Care System: A multi-step pain protocol including ultrasound-guided nerve anesthesia, EMLA cream, and algorithm-controlled PRESS injection equipment. Most patients report minimal to no discomfort.

3. Proven Track Record: Ranked No. 1 nationwide for Rejuran within 5 months of launch. SVIP-designated in just 3 months.

4. Full-Process Care: Every session includes 3D skin diagnosis, personalized treatment design, precision injection by the director personally, and post-treatment LDM or plasma care. Authentic Rejuran boxes and syringes are provided to every patient.

Why Book Through Creatrip

You pay the same price as booking directly &mdash; plus exclusive benefits. Every Creatrip customer who submits a review receives a complimentary Histolab Cis Mask Pack. Creatrip also provides a free Buddy service: a 14-day personal travel assistant (7 days before and after your appointment) with real-time English and Chinese support via WhatsApp and LINE.

Transparent Pricing, No Hidden Costs

Project U maintains honest pricing with no unnecessary consultation fees. Through Creatrip, you get premium Korean dermatology care at the best possible value.
