About

Clinic Introduction

Specialized dermatology clinic that reopened in 2025 after expanding into Seomyeon, Busan’s premier commercial district. We offer hotel-grade, refined interiors and a full set of globally validated high-end equipment, and our experienced medical team provides one-on-one customized consultation in the fields of lifting and Skin Booster. For international clients who want fast results and short recovery times during busy travel schedules, we operate optimized treatment protocols, making us a trusted representative clinic in Busan for medical tourists in terms of accessibility, scale, and expertise.

Why We Recommend It

1. Global standards, original equipment and genuine-product policy

Uses only globally proven, FDA-approved original devices such as Ulthera Prime, Thermage FLX, and Titanium (device). To completely eliminate the issue of counterfeit tips that overseas clients worry about most, we open the genuine tips in front of the client before the procedure and issue a certificate after the procedure. More than just owning the devices, we guarantee safe results with no need to worry about side effects during travel through customized design procedures based on analysis of each person’s anatomical structure.

2. Private personal space

For clients who dislike crowded, open-space 'factory-style clinics,' consultations and procedures are carried out in a one-person private room from start to finish.
By minimizing contact with others and providing professional care in a quiet setting, we offer the ultimate comfort for overseas VIP clients who value privacy.
3. Convenient Location
Located a 2-minute walk from Seomyeon Station (Lines 1 and 2), Busan’s largest downtown area and a major transit hub, minimizing travel time for tourists. 
Adjacent to Lotte Department Store, Jeonpo Cafe Street, and gourmet districts, you can use an hour between shopping and sightseeing for efficient beauty care. 
The perfect location for scheduling right after arriving in Busan or as your last stop before departure offers travelers the greatest convenience.
4. Dedicated interpreter service with no language barrier
To completely eliminate language barriers, full-time coordinators fluent in English, Japanese, and Chinese are available to support patients throughout their visit.
They accurately convey medical terminology in the patient’s language to ensure full understanding before procedures and carefully cover all aftercare instructions afterward.
On-site interpretation is provided approximately 3–4 days per week depending on the reservation schedule. When an on-site interpreter is unavailable, consultation and guidance are fully supported through our telephone interpretation system to ensure seamless communication.

How to Get There
