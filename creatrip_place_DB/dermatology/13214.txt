ULTRACLEAR KEY DOCTOR

Korea’s First Clinic to Introduce UltraClear (Key Doctor)

U ULTRACLEAR GUIDE View device features and 3D MIRACL technology ＋ From scars to skin tone and elasticity

An all-in-one premium laser for multiple concerns UltraClear Laser can improve pigmentation, acne scars, pores, brightening, wrinkles, and lifting in one treatment, making it especially suitable for people who want to address several skin concerns at once.

01 Precise energy delivery by skin depth Energy is delivered into deep skin layers—and to the SMAS layer if needed—to support collagen regeneration, elasticity, and lifting.

02 Skin tone, texture, and elasticity care together It addresses blemishes, redness, and pigmentation together while caring for tone, texture, and firmness. Visible, efficient results make it popular with international clients.

03 Six customizable modes by skin type Choose among six laser modes according to skin type and desired intensity, covering a wide range of ages from teens to people in their 70s.

3D MIRACL Technology 3D MIRACL (Minimally Invasive Regenerative Ablative Collagen Layering) is UltraClear’s proprietary technology, delivering ultra-fine energy deep into the skin to maximize collagen production for natural, long-lasting regeneration.
