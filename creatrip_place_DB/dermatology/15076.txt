How ONDA Lifting Boosts THERMAGE Results

Recently, &ldquo;preheating&rdquo; the skin before THERMAGE has been gaining attention as a way to maximize results. The idea isn&rsquo;t just to warm the surface but to deliver heat deep into the skin so THERMAGE&rsquo;s RF energy can work more effectively on targeted tissues.

However, devices often mentioned for preheating, like INMODE (FX, FORMA, etc.), only reach the upper dermis, limiting their ability to warm deeper layers. In contrast, the microwave-based ONDA can selectively deliver heat to both the dermis and subcutaneous fat layer, making it an excellent match for THERMAGE.

By using ONDA first to warm the deeper layers, THERMAGE&rsquo;s RF energy can act more efficiently. While THERMAGE and Ultherapy are often combined because they target different layers&mdash;Ultherapy works on the SMAS for lifting, and THERMAGE stimulates collagen in the dermis&mdash;ONDA and THERMAGE create a unique synergy.

Not only does ONDA enhance THERMAGE&rsquo;s efficiency through preheating, but it also offers its own benefits, such as fat layer stimulation and skin texture improvement, allowing for a more precise and strategic lifting & tightening plan.
